Akalapuzha Viewpoint

Akalapuzha is where the rhythm of the backwaters meets the silence of the soul. Known as the "hidden jewel" of Kozhikode, this vast expanse of water is fringed by dense, emerald-green coconut groves and ancient mangrove trails that breathe life into the horizon.

From the deck of Symphony, the view is a living painting. You will witness the sky bleeding into shades of molten gold and deep violet as the sun dips below the water line. The calm, glass-like surface reflects the soaring water birds and the gentle sway of the palms, offering a panoramic sanctuary far removed from the rush of the world.
